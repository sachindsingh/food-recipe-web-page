Scrambled Eggs with Avocado Toast


Ingredients:

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 slices of whole-grain bread
  • 1 avocado
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Scramble the eggs in a bowl and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Cook the scrambled eggs in a pan over medium heat until fluffy.
  3. Toast the bread slices.
  4. Slice the avocado and spread it on the toasted bread.
  5. Serve the scrambled eggs on top of the avocado toast.

Enjoy your flavorful Eggs with Avocado Toast!
