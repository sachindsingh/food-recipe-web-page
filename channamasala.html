Delicious Indian Chana Masala Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups Chickpeas (cooked or canned)
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable Oil
  • 1 large Onion, finely chopped
  • 2 Tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1 Green Chili, chopped
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh Coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  2. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chopped green chili. Sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and the oil separates.
  5. Add coriander powder, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Add cooked chickpeas and mix well. Cook for an additional 5-7 minutes.
  7. Sprinkle garam masala and garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  8. Serve hot with rice or naan.

Enjoy your flavorful Chana Masala!
