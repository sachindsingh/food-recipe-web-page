Pasta Salad Recipe

  • Home
  • Breakfast
  • Dinner

Ingredients:

  • 8 oz rotini pasta
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup black olives, sliced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the rotini pasta according to the package instructions. Drain and let it cool.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the cooked pasta, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, black olives, red onion, feta cheese, and fresh basil.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil and red wine vinegar. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Pour the dressing over the pasta salad and toss to combine.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld.
  6. Serve chilled and enjoy your delicious pasta salad!
