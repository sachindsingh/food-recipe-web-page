Veg Manchurian

Ingredients:

a
  • 1 cup finely chopped cabbage
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped capsicum
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onions
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons cornflour
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying
  • For the sauce:
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped garlic
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped ginger
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onions
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon chili sauce
  • 1 tablespoon tomato ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon cornflour mixed with 2 tablespoons water (for slurry)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix chopped cabbage, grated carrot, chopped capsicum, spring onions, all-purpose flour, cornflour, soy sauce, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, and salt.
  2. Shape the mixture into small balls and deep fry until golden brown. Keep aside.
  3. For the sauce, heat oil in a pan, sauté garlic, ginger, and spring onions.
  4. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, tomato ketchup, and the cornflour slurry. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the fried veg balls to the sauce, toss well until coated, and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.
  6. Season with salt and pepper, garnish with spring onions, and serve hot.

Enjoy your flavorful Veg Manchurian!
